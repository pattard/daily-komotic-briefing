# Komotic: trusted working context

Komotic is pre-beta. Foundational features are being built, not assumed already shipped.
It is a responsive engine and ecosystem for digital comics, not simply another reading app.
The engine adapts panels and lettering to different screens while preserving the artist's narrative intent.

Three products sit on that engine:
- Komotic Studio: creation.
- Marketplace: publishing and monetisation.
- Reader: reading and owning.

Komotic is a patronage business. Readers buy individual comics to back creators they follow and to own the work permanently. Artists keep 70% of net and set their own terms. Do not reinterpret this as 70% of gross sales or invent a definition of net.
The platform earns commissions on those sales and fees for optional exports purchased by artists when they want a rendered file of a published issue. Do not imply that readers are charged for these artist exports or that every purchase includes a downloadable file.

Responsive layout, automated lettering, text resizing, screen-reader support and localisation are strategically relevant. Do not assume all of these features are implemented.
Komotic does not provide animated content. Tweening/keyframes describe layout properties across different screen sizes, not animation over time.

The briefing's priorities are competitor awareness, opportunities and threats, and specific potential partnerships.
The initial focus is English-language markets, especially the US, UK and Canada. EU markets are also a launch priority. Asian and Latin American developments matter when they materially affect comics or these target markets; those markets are a slower initial expansion.
